말모이 Malmoi Korean words, gathered one by one

Learn Korean words a one-word translation can't explain.

Malmoi is for people who already speak Korean but keep running into words they only half-know. Look a word up in the sentence you found it in, keep it in your own word bank, and practice it by texting until it sticks.

What you can do

How it works

  1. Look it up where you found it. Paste the sentence, not just the word. Dictionaries list many senses; Malmoi explains the one in front of you.
  2. It's saved automatically. The word goes into your word bank along with that sentence, so you remember where it came from.
  3. Practice until it's stamped. Text drills bring words back on a review schedule. Get one right enough times and it earns the 익힘 (mastered) seal.

Why “Malmoi”?

말모이 means “gathering words.” It was the name of the first modern Korean dictionary manuscript, compiled word by word in the 1910s. This app does the same thing, one learner at a time.

Where the answers come from

Definitions come from the National Institute of Korean Language's Basic Korean Dictionary. Slang and rare words come from a live web search and are labeled “Web-sourced” with links. Anything that couldn't be checked against a dictionary is marked as unverified.
